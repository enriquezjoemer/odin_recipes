Spaghetti Recipe



Description

Spaghetti is a pasta dish made with wheat noodles and tomato sauce.


Ingredients

  • 1 pound spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese for serving

Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in crushed tomatoes, dried basil, and dried oregano.
  5. Simmer the sauce for 10-15 minutes.
  6. Drain the cooked spaghetti and toss with the sauce.
  7. Serve hot with grated Parmesan cheese.
