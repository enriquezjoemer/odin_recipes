Lasagna Recipe


Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. It's a hearty and comforting meal that's perfect for family dinners or special occasions.


Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned. Add the chopped onion and minced garlic, and cook until softened.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, dried basil, dried oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15-20 minutes.
  5. In a separate bowl, mix the ricotta cheese with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  6. In a baking dish, spread a layer of meat sauce on the bottom. Place a layer of cooked lasagna noodles on top, followed by a layer of ricotta cheese, then a layer of mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of meat sauce and a generous topping of mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  7. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 20-25 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  8. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
