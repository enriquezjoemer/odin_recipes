Pork Sinigang Recipe



Description

Pork Sinigang is a Filipino sour soup made with pork meat and various vegetables.


Ingredients

  • 1 pound pork belly, cut into chunks
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup tamarind soup
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup green beans, cut into pieces
  • 1 cup okra, cut into pieces
  • 1 cup tomatoes, cut into pieces

Steps

  1. Heat cooking oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add sliced onion and sauté for 2 minutes.
  3. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute.
  4. Add pork chunks and cook until browned.
  5. Pour in tamarind soup and water.
  6. Add fish sauce, sugar, salt, and pepper.
  7. Simmer the soup for 30-40 minutes until the pork is tender.
